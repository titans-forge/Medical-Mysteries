# Medical Mysteries 0.7.0 — Investigation Edition

Released October 17, 2026. Edition identifier: **FG2-MEDICAL-MYSTERIES-0.7.0-20261017**.

Six guided stories and three investigation families, now with six current arrivals. Paired arrivals require learners to weigh different evidence, explain a conclusion or justified uncertainty, communicate patient changes and keep a structured handoff current.
